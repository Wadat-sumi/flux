# Flux data sources

The built-in intersection, flyover, bottleneck and ramp presets are synthetic educational examples, with no real-city calibration claim. Poisson arrivals and vehicle attributes are generated deterministically from the recorded seed and mean OD rates.

The full-stack revision adds uploaded OpenStreetMap JSON and observed OD CSV ingestion. Each accepted import retains exact raw text, normalized scenario, declared source/license/capture time, SHA-256 and assumptions in the owner's dataset library. Imported observations become constant mean rates; actual arrivals are simulated, not a replay of measured trajectories. Native scenario JSON/device saves omit this lineage; server saves and result reports retain it.

public/data/bengaluru-osm.json is a small real subset of OSM way 46728546 and its dependent nodes, downloaded through the public OSM API on the timestamp recorded in the file. Source URL: https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map.json?bbox=77.5936,12.9756,77.5952,12.9770 . © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . It contains map geometry/tags only and zero assumed traffic counts. The raw subset is distributable under ODbL with attribution; it is not a whole-city calibrated network.

OSM imports preserve direction/layer topology and metric projection, use reviewed/inferred speed/lane defaults with warnings, and do not infer turn restrictions, detailed signal stages or cross-layer ramp connectivity. Demand CSV must describe OD motor-vehicle trips with matching Flux source/sink IDs, nonnegative integer vehicle counts, a positive exposure interval and ISO UTC observation time. Unsupported columns, overlapping intervals, missing roles/routes and bounds fail validation. PCU/link counts are not silently treated as OD vehicle counts.

The owner selected Bengaluru, Mumbai, Chennai, Pune, Hyderabad and Delhi. real-data-sources.md records public report/Kaggle/Snowflake/TomTom research with provenance, licensing and access gaps. data-request.md specifies a minimum corridor survey. No verified matched observed OD/signal/validation package for all six cities has been acquired. No commercial data or report tables have been republished under an assumed license.

TomTom live segment observations are optional, server-side, vault-authenticated and transient. Contract fixtures are explicitly synthetic. No live response/coverage is claimed yet. Current speeds/travel times are provider estimates, not OD counts, legal speed limits or actual signal-controller plans. Retention/reuse requires the appropriate agreement; observations are excluded from database/report export by default.
